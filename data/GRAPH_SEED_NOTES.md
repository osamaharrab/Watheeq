# Watheeq graph seed

Synthetic corporate ownership and fractional asset records supplied by Watheeq for candidate assessment. These are not real companies, persons, assets or instruments and must not be cited as such.

Files are newline-delimited JSON. Field names are given in `reference/graph_schema_registry.json`, which is the authoritative description of the queryable surface.

No further description of the contents is provided. The records have not been cleaned.
